Keep in mind

💪 Don't live at the gym. Get your work done, focus on quality reps, and recover.

🥤 Eat enough, hydrate, and get protein through food or a shake if it helps you meet your needs.

🧠 Focus on the muscle you're training, keep your form controlled, and don't chase weight at the expense of technique.

🎯 Long-term goal: work toward a muscle-up. Calisthenics is a challenging next step.

THE PROGRAM

Your workout plan

Start light, learn the movements, and build up gradually. Your routine is editable.

Training notes

• Your first sessions should be lighter while you learn the movements.

• Rest between sets as needed. Keep reps controlled and stop if you feel sharp pain, dizziness, or unusual symptoms.

• Training to failure is optional, not required. Leave a little in the tank while learning.

• Allow recovery between hard sessions. Soreness is not the goal or a reliable measure of progress.
